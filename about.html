Behind the Lens

About

Sultan Siddique photographs mountains, canyons and the animals that live in them. Most of this work comes from the Great Smoky Mountains and the Blue Ridge, where fall color, valley fog and the elk rut bring him back each October.

Further west, the collections follow Yosemite Valley after a spring storm, the Golden Gate in summer fog, and the slot canyons and overlooks of northern Arizona and southern Utah.
